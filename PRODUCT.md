# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual web users who want to convert a YouTube video to MP3 or MP4 quickly, often while doing something else. They arrive with a link, expect the converter to be obvious, and may use secondary features such as games, music discovery, previews, lyrics, or the time-sync studio while they wait for a conversion to finish.

## Product Purpose

YT Converter helps users paste a YouTube link, choose an output format, track conversion progress, and download the resulting file with minimal friction. Success means the main conversion path stays clear in the first viewport, wait-time features feel useful or fun without interrupting the task, and every state makes the next action easy to understand.

## Brand Personality

Playful, fast, music-oriented. The voice should feel casual and direct: cool enough to make waiting less dull, but restrained enough that users trust the converter and are not distracted from the download workflow.

## Anti-references

Avoid interfaces that feel overloaded, noisy, or visually exhausting. The product should not look like a cluttered download portal, a banner-heavy media site, or an arcade page where games and effects compete with conversion. Secondary tools should stay secondary until the user chooses them.

## Design Principles

1. Keep conversion dominant.
2. Make waiting feel lighter, not busier.
3. Use themes as atmosphere, not distraction.
4. Make every async state explain what is happening.
5. Preserve trust through readable controls and predictable interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast across all supported themes, and reduced-motion behavior for decorative animation. The interface should remain understandable without relying on color alone, motion alone, or visual density.
